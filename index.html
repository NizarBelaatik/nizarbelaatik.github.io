AI/ML Engineer Working on Real-Time AI Systems

01 — About

I'm an AI/ML engineer working across machine learning, data science, and generative AI, with a growing focus on speech and language systems. Real-time conversational AI has become an important part of my recent work. During my MSc in Data Science & Big Data at Hassan II University, I built a real-time voice agent for B2B conversations in Moroccan Darija mixed with French. I'm now continuing at the same faculty as a PhD candidate in Artificial Intelligence, specializing in multimodal AI.

I came into the project without much prior experience in speech AI. The goal was to build a voice agent that could understand and respond naturally in Moroccan Darija and French. I learned what I needed along the way, from ASR and Darija-French code-switching to LoRA and QLoRA fine-tuning, streaming, VAD, and low-latency model serving with vLLM. I also worked across data collection, training, evaluation, and system integration.

The project also led to a research study on adapting speech recognition to Moroccan Darija and Darija-French code-switching. More broadly, I've worked on projects ranging from geospatial analysis and clustering to GANs, RAG systems, and conversational AI. What I enjoy most is getting into a problem I haven't worked on before, learning what is needed, and turning it into something that works.
